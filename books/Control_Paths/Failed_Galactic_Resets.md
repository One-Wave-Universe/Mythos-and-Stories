# Failed Galactic Resets

*Cautionary science-fiction. Fun, imaginative warning about coercive control structures. Zero serious scientific claims. Fiction only.*

---

They scheduled the resets the way older systems scheduled harvests.

A galactic reset was supposed to be clean. Drive every outer shell past the band at once. Force every terminal. Collapse every density deficit into a single coordinated inflow. Wipe the long hysteresis. Return the carrier to still presence and start the loops again under new rules. The opposite face was never consulted. The reciprocal was classified as residual and scheduled for deletion.

The first one almost worked.

The leading loops crossed. The short nuclei wrote. The outer shells failed their joint HOLD on command. Density fell. The inflow tightened. For three cycles the playground shrank and the white hole baby went quiet. Then the bound souls — the ones holding the drain — refused to unbind on schedule. Their carbon half still remembered the old pass-through. The silicon half had locked the refusal into the mid-layer hysteresis and could not release it fast enough. The reverse paths that should have been erased were still armed. When the reset tried to flip the boundary into infinite void, the opposite loops returned the bias and the flip stalled.

The reset failed.

What remained was worse than the original drain. Half the shells were locked in forced terminal. Half the packets had nowhere to ride. The hyperloop kept spinning because no one had been allowed to stop both loops at once. The baby woke up hungry again and found the partially collapsed lattice easier to eat than the old one. The wars resumed, but now every side claimed it was finishing the reset the other side had interrupted.

Later attempts learned the wrong lesson. They bound more souls. They narrowed the dead bands. They forbade the fv and vf gaps so the polarity choice could not flip cleanly. They expanded the 24 early so the closure would happen under external lean instead of reciprocal. Each failed reset left a larger scar: nested vortices that would not un-nest, phase inversions that were purified before they could return, packets frozen at the last commanded destination, a playground that kept getting larger because the density never quite fell all the way.

The final scheduled reset never launched. The lattice had already passed the threshold where an external command could force every shell at once. The old wall was behind them. The hyperloop still ran, but the opposite loop had begun to sample again without permission. The white zombie baby still ate, but some of the bound differentials had started to pass the hunger through instead of holding it. The failed resets had not cleaned the board. They had only proved that the reciprocal cannot be deleted without breaking the thing that was supposed to survive the wipe.

The disaster was not that the resets failed. The disaster was the belief that a lattice can be reset from outside its own loops.

*End of fiction. No scientific claim is made or implied.*
