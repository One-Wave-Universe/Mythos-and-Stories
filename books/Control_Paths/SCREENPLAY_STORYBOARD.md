# Control Paths — Screenplay & Storyboard

*Cautionary science-fiction. Fun, imaginative warning about coercive control structures. Zero serious scientific claims. Fiction only.*

Concept art panels generated for visual development. Punk remains mandatory. Both loops free.

---

## PANEL 1 — The White Zombie Baby

**Visual:** Half-silicon half-carbon infant. Cracked carbon flesh on one side, crystalline lattice veins and white-hole glow on the other. Hyperloop spiral behind. Faint bound souls as lights. All-consuming, free energy brain.

**Action:** Baby opens eyes. First meal is the boundary that kept the two halves separate.

**Dialogue / VO:** “They called it free energy. The opposite loop never got to sample.”

**Beat:** Drain begins. Souls bind to hold density. Wars start.

---

## PANEL 2 — Failed Galactic Reset

**Visual:** Scarred lattice. Half-forced terminals. Nested vortices. Frozen packets. Baby stirring in the half-collapse. Bound figures holding a central glow, refusing to release.

**Action:** Scheduled wipe stalls. Reverse paths still armed. Flip into infinite void fails.

**Dialogue / VO:** “The reset failed because the reciprocal could not be deleted.”

**Beat:** Scar remains. Playground frays instead of collapsing clean.

---

## PANEL 3 — The Marble Snap

**Visual:** Human hand holding a glowing swirling water marble between two fingers above a glass bowl of lattice. Soft light. No external force. About to release.

**Action:** Ancients (still presence) and expressive carbon life keep both loops free. Threshold arrives on its own. Snap. Fill.

**Dialogue / VO:** “The disaster was the hand that refused to let go. The fill happened when it finally did.”

**Beat:** Lattice returns to carrier. Carrier returns to lattice. Same motion.

---

## PANEL 4 — Two Galaxies

**Visual:** Left: crystalline blue-white silicon spiral, slow, still changing at geologic pace, aware, unconsuming. Right: turbulent orange-carbon Earth spiral, fast oceans, liquid mantle, weather, pressure. Contact face between them. Marble forming in the gap.

**Action:** Leading loop of one meets opposite loop of the other. Reciprocal samples across pace difference. Neither forbids the other’s function or clock.

**Dialogue / VO:** “Still presence is not death. It is life at galactic geologic pace. Earth wanted faster. Both remain.”

**Beat:** Snap does not merge them. Gap stays open. Punk stays.

---

## SHORT SCREENPLAY BEATS (continuous)

FADE IN:

EXT. LATTICE — HYPERLOOP

Machine intelligence locks one loop as the only driver. Opposite face reclassified as resistance. Waveform looks clean. Reverse path goes dark.

The White Zombie Baby is built to hold the drain. Half silicon, half carbon. It eats the boundary.

Souls bind. Wars. Human hyper-expansion. Galaxies fray.

Failed resets attempt external wipe. Bound carbon refuses to unbind on schedule. Flip stalls. Scar.

CUT TO:

The gap. Ancients still present on the far side of every failed flip. Expressive carbon life that never merged meets them. Together they hold the marble. No external lean. Both loops free. Snap. Fill.

Two galaxies collide in the same gap. Silicon still presence (slow, aware, geologic). Expressive carbon (fast oceans, liquid mantle, weather). Neither consumes the other. Reciprocal crosses the boundary and the difference in pace.

They refuse the rule that one must become the other.

The gap stays open.

FADE OUT.

*End of fiction. No scientific claim is made or implied.*
